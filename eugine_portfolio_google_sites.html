About Me

Background, expertise & what drives me

Turning Data Into Decisions

I'm a results-driven Data and Policy Analyst based in Kisumu, Kenya, with a strong foundation in econometrics, statistical modelling, and quantitative research. My work spans agricultural data analysis, monetary policy research, and performance optimisation — always with the goal of delivering clear, actionable insights.

Currently serving as a Graduate Analyst at Savannah Seritech, I've successfully reduced silkworm mortality by 75% and optimised rearing conditions to achieve consistent 1.2 kg+ yields per 1,000 silkworms. I also led analysis of 196+ Bambara nut accessions, identifying top-performing varieties that directly informed procurement strategy.

With a BA in Economics and Mathematics from Egerton University and ongoing Data Science training at ALX Africa, I bring both academic rigour and practical field experience to every project.
